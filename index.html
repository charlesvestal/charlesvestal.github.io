Firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1

Each one installs from its page in the browser, and you can go back to M-VAVE's firmware from there too.

Grooveboxes

  • X0X 909, 808, two 303s and a breakbeat player, with song mode and motion
  • LOOM-8 inspired by the OP-XY: eight tracks on fifteen engines, step tricks and locks, weave, a conductor and throws
  • Polly a pocket arranger inspired by the Yamaha QY70: a band of eight follows the chords you play

Instruments

  • FoMni-1 a chord harp: strum the white keys, pick chords on the black ones
  • MONK-1 twelve singers, played with velocity and pressure from the FM-1's own keys
  • AMB-1 a generative ambient machine, inspired by Music for Airports
  • OCA-1 an ocarina for games that listen: the twelve songs of the Switch 2 Ocarina of Time, played to the console's microphone

Controllers

  • CTL-1 a MIDI controller: notes in any scale, four banks of knobs, an arpeggiator and Euclidean drums, with General MIDI sounds of its own

Games

  • Boardwalk a seaside arcade of music games: seventeen cabinets, from change ringing to a roller coaster whose track is the melody, with tickets and a prize counter

Make your own

  • Ripple an FM-1 you reprogram without reflashing: instruments in Lua that play sounds in Faust or C, added over a USB drive
  • fm1-base the open base and template for writing FM-1 firmware in C, with a simulator, a browser emulator, Bluetooth MIDI and USB audio
  • Mocky pictures on the FM-1's screen, sent from the browser, for checking screen mockups on the real hardware

Tools

  • FM-1 Screen Reader hear what the FM-1's screen says, with your own screen reader or the page's voice

Source only

  • Velo velocity and aftertouch from the FM-1's keys, with no hardware changes
  • Radio internet radio over WiFi: every place on Radio Garden, with punch-in effects

Schwung for the Ableton Move

An open framework for adding synths, effects and tools to the Move.

  • schwung.dev browse the modules: synths, effects, MIDI tools and standalone instruments
  • Schwung on GitHub the framework itself, with the docs for writing a module
  • Schwung Installer install Schwung and its modules on your Move from Mac, Windows or Linux

Apps

Small, characterful music apps. For all of them, see charles.pizza/apps.

  • NuSaw iOS · Mac a polyphonic supersaw synth: up to 25 detuned saws, filter, envelopes, chorus and delay
  • QWERTET iOS · AUv3 a computer-keyboard MIDI controller with four layouts, one-key chords and an arpeggiator
  • Ambiotica iOS · Mac · Windows · Linux a generative ambient processor: a rolling looper, a granular cloud, micro-loops and reverb
  • TAPESCAM iOS · Mac · Windows · Linux lo-fi drive, tape wobble and dropouts, inspired by the four-track
  • Punchline iOS · AUv3 a playful drum machine with automation, punch-in effects and a sequenced gate
